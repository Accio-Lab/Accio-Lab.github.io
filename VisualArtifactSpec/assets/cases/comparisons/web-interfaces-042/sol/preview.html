The ideal model

s = n₁ sin θ₁ / n₂

If s > 1 + 10⁻¹⁰, total internal reflection occurs and no refraction angle is defined. Otherwise, θ₂ = asin(min(1, s)). At the critical point, θ₂ = 90°.

A critical angle, θc = asin(n₂ / n₁), exists only when n₁ > n₂. During total internal reflection, the reflected angle equals the incident angle.
